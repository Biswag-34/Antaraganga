# Visual and functional review

Review date: 23 October 2026.

## Reference review

All ten supplied boards were visually inspected: one system board and nine page-type boards. All page layouts are implemented as HTML/CSS/React with local responsive media. No board screenshot is embedded in the website.

## Intentional differences

- The supplied concept wordmark is used; its artwork differs slightly from the logo drawn in the visualizations.
- Clinical images are the mapped pack assets. Some board photographs are different compositions; no additional photographs were fabricated to recreate them.
- Named leadership cards are replaced by a team enquiry pending verification. Institutional documentation, videos and procurement records are not fabricated.
- DOCX download labels replace PDF labels because the pack provides DOCX originals.
- Current services and proposed departments are not presented as verified operational facts. Funding allocations are labelled planning estimates; live remaining balances are omitted.
- Mobile services include a scrollable service chooser to make every service reachable. The selected service appears first, following the reference's focus on one image/content panel.
- The mobile footer preserves site navigation rather than hiding useful links to match the abbreviated phone footer exactly.
- The contact review offers a local draft download because no delivery service was provided.
- Health-card benefits are not inferred. The demo card and proposed process remain clearly identified.
- No illustrative map is presented as a verified map or working branch network.
- Gold controls use navy text to improve small-text contrast; this deliberately differs from the reference's white-on-gold labels.
- Programme scope hotspots explain general source categories; images are not final room or procurement specifications.
